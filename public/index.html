Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know before your first visit.

How do I book a slot?

Open the Branches page, pick the branch you want, and tap Book. You'll get a confirmation and a reminder before your slot.

What's the difference between Starter and Elite?

Starter covers slot booking, Smart Key entry, and access to every branch. Elite adds no-reserve entry, parking, and priority access at busy branches.

Can I switch branches or cancel anytime?

Yes — your membership works at every HUMF branch, and you can cancel anytime from My Account with no lock-in contract.

How does the Smart Key work?

Once you join, your Smart Key appears in the app. Hold your phone near any branch door to unlock it — no physical card needed.

Is HUMF available outside Bangkok?

We're expanding branch by branch across Thailand — check the Branches page for the latest locations near you.
